Blackberry Dumpling Recipe

Revived By: Sonja Norwood
Presented By: Daria Lindsey

Traditional Black American recipes are dissappearing due to loss of oral tradition, decline of "scratch" cooking and commercialization and cultural erasure.

Many recipes were born out of want, need & celebration just as others were created out of desperation and survival.

Historically, it was illegal for enslaved people to learn to read or write. Because of this, culinary techniques snd recipes were passed down entirely through oral tradition.

Modern food systems favor convenience over labor-intensive preparation. Ancestral cooking required patience and often-times hours of foraging, slow-cooking, and preservation such as curing or pickling

Southern and "Soul Food" have been largely commercialized, the roots of these dishes often scrubbed away.

Many foundational recipes relied on using the "whole animal". Today, mass-market grocery stores make finding authentic ingredients physically harder to find.

Viral Food Creator, Artist, History Educator Sonja Norwood

During Black History Month of this year, Sonja Norwood took on the remarkable feat of reviving dozens of nearly forgotten Black American recipes. Thorough and meticulous, she shares the recipes, context and history behind each recipe.
